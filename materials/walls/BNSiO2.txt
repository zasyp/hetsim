# Borosil BN-SiO2 (BNSiO2) -- Hall thruster channel wall.
# The standard SPT-family channel ceramic (e.g. Fakel BGP-10).
#
# Secondary-electron-emission (SEE) yield, Maxwellian-averaged over the
# incident electron energy distribution:
#
#     gamma(Te) = gamma_2plusb * a * Te^b        (Te in eV)
#
# a, b are the coefficients of the mono-energetic power-law fit
# delta(E) = a * E^b; gamma_2plusb = Gamma(2 + b) is the gamma-function
# factor that appears when that yield is averaged over a Maxwellian EEDF.
#
# Coefficients from Goebel & Katz, "Fundamentals of Electric Propulsion"
# (Wiley/JPL, 2008), Ch. 7, Table 7-1 and Eq. 7.3-30.
# Underlying mono-energetic BN-SiO2 measurements: N. Gascon, M. Dudeck &
# S. Barral, "Wall material effects in stationary plasma thrusters. I,"
# Phys. Plasmas 10, 4123 (2003).
#
# key            value
a                0.123
b                0.528
gamma_2plusb     1.36
